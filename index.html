About the Project

Background and motivation

Math fact fluency, the ability to recall basic facts such as multiplication and division quickly and accurately, is a building block for the multi-digit arithmetic, fractions, and problem solving that 4th graders take on. Building that fluency takes regular practice, but traditional drills like flash cards and worksheets can feel repetitive, and students often lose interest before the facts become automatic. At the same time, more and more classwork happens on computers, so typing is becoming an everyday skill that young students rarely get a chance to practice on purpose. qw3rty brings these two needs together: it turns math fact practice into a fast-paced typing race, so students build arithmetic recall and keyboard skills in the same activity, and they want to keep playing.

Who it's for

qw3rty is designed for 4th grade students in Massachusetts, along with the teachers who want an engaging way to reinforce math facts in class or at home. Its content is guided by the grade 4 standards in the Massachusetts Curriculum Framework for Mathematics.

How it works

Players join a race and are shown a series of math problems. To move their racer forward, they type the correct answer as quickly as they can. Accurate, quick answers push a player ahead, so success depends on knowing the facts and typing them efficiently. The game runs in a web browser and is built with Vite and React.
